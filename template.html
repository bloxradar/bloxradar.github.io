Roblox trending charts · working codes

    How it works

    Tap any game to see its working codes (tap a code to copy), how to redeem them, and a play link. The star saves a game to Favorites — stored in your browser only.

    Charts come from every discovery row Roblox exposes — the five main charts plus genre trending, top earning, top rated, and most popular (games down to 500 players feed All Games and search).

    Codes & freshness

    Codes are gathered from outlets that verify them and re-checked daily. Codes are often case-sensitive and can expire within hours — if one fails, check the game's official Discord.

    Search Buzz compares each top game's Google search interest over the last 24 hours with its own 7-day average — momentum on the wider internet, not just inside Roblox.
